          About this project

          The Untold Stories of Fulɓe is a cultural heritage and storytelling project. It records the experiences, histories, knowledge and traditions of Fulɓe people.

          We publish researched features, oral histories, personal essays and community contributions. Experiences and traditions differ across regions and families, and we welcome thoughtful discussion.

          Share your perspective

          When we preserve our stories, we give future generations more than memories. We give them context, belonging and a chance to understand.

          Your story matters

          Do you have a family memory, historical account, photograph or piece of knowledge worth preserving? Students, elders, researchers, artists and community members are all welcome.
