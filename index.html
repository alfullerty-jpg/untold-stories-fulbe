Why this platform exists

About this platform

The Untold Stories of Fulɓe is a cultural heritage and storytelling project dedicated to recording the diverse experiences, histories, knowledge and traditions of Fulɓe people.

We publish researched features, oral histories, personal essays and community contributions. We welcome thoughtful discussion and recognise that experiences and traditions differ across regions and families.
